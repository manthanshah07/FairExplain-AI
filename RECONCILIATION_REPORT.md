## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the single authoritative technical baseline for FairExplain AI by reconciling the seven preliminary audit documents located in `product-audit/` against the verified source code in the repository.

### Key Audit Finding
The FairExplain AI codebase exhibits an acute **architectural duality**:
1. **Mathematical Domain Core is Production-Grade:** The inner financial accounting and eligibility rule engine ([`backend/app/domain/`](file:///Users/manthanshah/Documents/FairExplain/FairExplain-AI/backend/app/domain)) is implemented in pure, dependency-free Python using `Decimal` arithmetic with strict `ROUND_HALF_UP` quantization. All 70 domain unit tests pass in 0.03s. It correctly enforces 3-state eligibility (`ELIGIBLE`, `INELIGIBLE`, `PENDING_EVIDENCE`), ascending priority queues, and non-negative tuition invariants. Generative AI is strictly isolated and barred from altering financial numbers or eligibility booleans.
2. **Security & Outer Perimeter is Non-Existent:** The outer HTTP and persistence layers have **zero authentication**, **zero authorization**, and **zero tenant isolation**. The backend creates student accounts with a hardcoded password string (`demo_hashed_password`), serves student dossiers to unauthenticated requests via IDOR/BOLA, stores uploaded identity documents in ephemeral local disk storage (`./uploads`), and generates SHA-256 PDF hashes that are neither cryptographically signed nor stored in the database.

### Release Verdict
* **Ready to Begin Implementation:** **YES** (The domain core and database schemas provide a solid foundation).
* **Ready for Internal Testing:** **NO** (Blocked by lack of authentication and Broken Object-Level Authorization).
* **Ready for Controlled Institutional Pilot:** **NO** (Unconditionally blocked until Milestones 1 through 5 are 100% completed and verified).
* **Ready for Commercial Production:** **NO** (Blocked by enterprise SLA, high availability, and SOC2 requirements).

## 4. Statutory Legal Compliance vs. Proposed Retention Policies

A critical outcome of this reconciliation is distinguishing between **verified statutory obligations** under Indian law and **proposed operational engineering rules**:

1. **Statutory Principles under India DPDP Act 2023 (Verified Statutory Law):**
   - Section 6: Notice and consent must be unconditional, itemized, and available in English and 22 scheduled languages.
   - Section 8(6): Mandatory notification of personal data breaches to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected individuals without delay.
   - Section 8(7): Data Fiduciary **must erase personal data** as soon as the specified purpose is no longer served or upon consent withdrawal. *The statute contains NO explicit 180-day figure.*
   - Aadhaar Act 2016 (Section 29) & UIDAI Regulations: Raw, unmasked 12-digit Aadhaar numbers and photocopies must never be stored on uncertified servers.
2. **Proposed Operational Engineering Policy (FairExplain AI Rule):**
   - Retain student income proofs and marksheets during the active admission semester (120 days) plus a 60-day appeal window (total: 180 days post-award).
   - At Day 180, an automated background worker permanently deletes physical PDF documents from S3.
   - The platform permanently retains: (a) anonymized calculation inputs, (b) eligibility outcome booleans, (c) immutable audit log entries, and (d) cryptographic SHA-256 hashes of the deleted files.
3. **Specific Claims Requiring Qualified Legal Counsel & DPO Review:**
   - *Claim 1:* "Purging tax records after 180 days satisfies all regulatory requirements." -> **Requires Legal Review** against General Financial Rules (GFR, 2017) and UGC/AICTE accounting norms, which require financial disbursement vouchers to be retained for 3 to 8 years.
   - *Claim 2:* "Cryptographic SHA-256 digests of erased student documents do not constitute personal data." -> **Requires Legal Review** to confirm whether high-entropy hashes of student documents are legally deemed anonymized under DPDP Act rules.
   - *Claim 3:* "Cloud hosting on AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) satisfies all data sovereignty rules." -> **Requires Legal Review** regarding multi-region backup replication and institutional government contracts.
   - *Claim 4:* "Collecting parent ITR income certificates is covered by applicant student consent." -> **Requires Legal Review** regarding third-party non-applicant data principals (parents/guardians) under DPDP Act Section 6.
